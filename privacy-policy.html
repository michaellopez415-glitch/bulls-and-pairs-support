Bulls and Pairs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Bulls and Pairs ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Bulls and Pairs mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (or other login credentials) when you create an account.
  • Identity verification information: information submitted to our identity verification provider to confirm you meet our age and identity requirements.
  • Profile content: photos and other content you choose to upload to your profile.
  • Messages: content you send to other users through in-app messaging.
  • Approximate location: general (city-level) location, used to show distance to other users and suggest nearby matches. You can use the App without sharing this information, though some matching features may be limited.
  • Payment and subscription information: if you purchase a subscription, our payment processing partner shares limited transaction data with us (such as subscription status and renewal dates). We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify your identity and age eligibility
  • Provide core app functionality, including matching, messaging, and distance-based suggestions
  • Process subscription payments and manage your subscription status
  • Maintain the security of the App and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Respond to support requests

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share information with the following third-party service providers, who process it on our behalf:

  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases and status across the App Store and Google Play.
  • Supabase — provides our backend database and account infrastructure, and stores account, profile, and message data on our behalf.
  • Identity verification provider — processes identity documents or information submitted during account verification.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process app purchases and, where applicable, sign-in.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with advertisers or advertising networks.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App's features. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Account deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by visiting our Account Deletion page.
  • Location: location sharing is optional; disabling it will limit distance-based matching features but will not prevent App use.
  • Access and correction: you may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request its deletion. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

6. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Age Requirement

Bulls and Pairs is intended solely for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Identity verification is used to help enforce this requirement.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
