Bulls and Pairs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Bulls and Pairs ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Bulls and Pairs mobile application and the website bullsandpairs.com (together, the "Service"), a dating and social platform for adults in the ethical non-monogamy and lifestyle community. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (or other login credentials) when you create an account.
  • Profile content: photos, bio, preferences, and other details you choose to add to your profile, including details about you and any partner profile you link.
  • Identity and age verification: we use a third-party identity verification provider (Didit) to confirm you are 18 or older. The provider processes your identification documents on our behalf. We receive only your verification status (verified or not verified) and do not store copies of your government ID.
  • Messages: content you send to other users through in-app messaging.
  • Approximate location: general (city-level) location, used to show distance to other users and suggest nearby matches. You can use the Service without sharing this information, though some matching features may be limited.
  • Payment and subscription information: if you purchase a subscription, our payment processing partners share limited transaction data with us (such as subscription status and renewal dates). We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Usage information (analytics): if you accept analytics cookies on our website, we use Google Analytics to collect page views, browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with our site. We do not send your name, email, profile content, or messages to Google Analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify your identity and age eligibility
  • Provide core functionality, including matching, messaging, and distance-based suggestions
  • Show your profile to other users
  • Process subscription payments and manage your subscription status
  • Maintain the safety and security of the Service, investigate reports, and enforce our Community Guidelines
  • Send account-related and transactional emails, such as password resets
  • Understand how our website is used and improve it (only if you accept analytics cookies)
  • Respond to support requests

3. How We Share Information

We share information with the following parties:

  • Other users: your profile, including photos and other details you choose to share, is visible to other users of the Service, as is standard for a dating platform.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases and status across the App Store and Google Play.
  • Supabase — provides our backend database and account infrastructure, and stores account, profile, and message data on our behalf.
  • Didit (identity verification provider) — processes identity documents or information submitted during account verification.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process app purchases and, where applicable, sign-in.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — provides website usage statistics, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Law enforcement or legal process: where required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or advertising networks.

4. Sensitive Personal Information

Some information on Bulls and Pairs may be considered "sensitive personal information" under California law, such as your approximate geolocation and information that could reveal sexual orientation, relationship structure, or sexual preferences through your profile, photos, messages, or stated preferences. We use this information only to operate core matching, browsing, and messaging features of the Service. California residents have the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information; see Section 8 below.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. When you first visit, you are asked to Accept or Decline analytics cookies through a banner. If you decline, Google Analytics does not load or track your visit. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, investigate safety reports, and enforce our agreements. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Data Security

All data transmitted between the Service and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We restrict access to our databases and take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Account deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by visiting our Account Deletion page.
  • Location: location sharing is optional; disabling it will limit distance-based matching features but will not prevent use of the Service.
  • Analytics: you can accept or decline analytics cookies at any time (see Section 5).
  • Access and correction: you may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Request a copy of it
  • Request deletion of it
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise these rights, email bullsandpairs@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by California law.

9. Age Requirement

Bulls and Pairs is intended solely for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Identity verification is used to enforce this requirement. If you misrepresent your age or attempt to bypass identity verification, your account will be terminated.

10. Safety

Your safety matters to us. We verify identity and age through our verification provider, but we do not conduct criminal background checks on users. We encourage you to use caution when communicating with or meeting other users, including meeting in public places and telling a friend or family member about your plans. Report any user who violates our Community Guidelines or makes you feel unsafe using the in-app report feature or by emailing bullsandpairs@gmail.com.
