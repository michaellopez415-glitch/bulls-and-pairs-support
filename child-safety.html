Child Safety Standards

Bulls and Pairs is an adults-only platform (18+) for the ethical non-monogamy and lifestyle community. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind, and we take proactive steps to prevent minors from accessing or being depicted on our platform.

Age Verification

Every user must confirm they are 18 years of age or older during sign-up. In addition, every profile must complete government ID verification, including a liveness check, before it becomes visible to other users in Browse. Profiles that have not completed ID verification are not shown to other users.

Zero Tolerance Policy

We strictly prohibit any content, communication, or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or otherwise harms minors. Any account found to violate this policy is immediately and permanently banned, and any content is removed without delay.

Reporting

Users can report any profile, message, or content directly within the app using our built-in report feature. Reports are reviewed by our team, and appropriate action — including account suspension or termination — is taken promptly.
